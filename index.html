08FAQ
Why choose Goldfish Socials? +
We don't treat projects as tickets. Every piece we deliver is something we'd proudly put in our own portfolio. We combine cinematic craft with strategic thinking — your content will look great and actually work.
What's your typical turnaround? +
Most projects are delivered within 3–7 business days depending on scope. Rush delivery is available. We respect deadlines — always.
How does pricing work? +
We price per project, not per hour. You'll know the full cost before we start. Contact us for a custom quote — we work with budgets of all sizes.
How many revisions are included? +
All projects include 2 rounds of revisions. We've found that with our briefing process, most clients never use both. Additional rounds are available if needed.
What formats do you deliver in? +
Whatever you need: H.264/H.265, ProRes, DNxHD for video. PDF, AI, SVG for graphics. We deliver production-ready files optimized for your platform.
